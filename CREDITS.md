# Credits

WP Title Layer is an independent clean-room-style rewrite designed around a
different title and series model.

Compatibility behavior was informed by static review of Secondary Title 2.2.1
by Kolja Nolte, distributed under GPL-2.0-or-later. WP Title Layer preserves
interoperability with the legacy `_secondary_title` metadata key and selected
public interfaces. It does not claim to be an official continuation of
Secondary Title.

- Reviewed version: `2.2.1`
- Author: Kolja Nolte
